* ADR-4 Machines ask the record keeper through ACP elicitation

** Context

ADR-2 Talk to machines over ACP has a machine's questions reach the IDE as ACP
messages. A machine that asks in its message text ends its turn, and the IDE
cannot tell that turn from the end of its work. ACP elicitation lets a machine
ask in the middle of a turn: the machine sends a form, the IDE shows it, and
the record keeper's answers return to the machine as JSON. The IDE opts in by
advertising elicitation when it starts a session. Claude Code and Codex offer
a device for asking questions, which their ACP adapters carry as elicitation;
Codex offers it only in its plan mode. agent-shell shows elicitation forms
behind an experimental switch, in form mode only.

** Decision

The IDE advertises form elicitation to every machine, and instructs each
machine to ask the record keeper only through the device its AI program offers
for questions.

** Status

Accepted.

** Consequences

- A machine's questions are answered within its turn.
- The record keeper answers in a form: a choice among options, or text.
- A machine on an AI program without a device for questions does not ask the
  record keeper.
- The IDE depends on agent-shell's experimental elicitation, and follows its
  changes.
- Elicitation in url mode goes unanswered until agent-shell shows it.
