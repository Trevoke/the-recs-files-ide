#+TITLE: Vision

* What it is for

The IDE is an agentic integrated product delivery environment, where the record
keeper maintains the truth of the target product through record management, and
where the target product's codebase is reconciled with the records.

* Who it serves

Record keepers: the people working on a target product whose records are the
source of truth for its code, in the way the-recs-files defines it.

* The domain and its bounded contexts

The domain is a target product's records and the reconciliation of its codebase
with them.

- Records :: the record keeper's work on the records.
- Implementation :: agents turning record changes into code, and the record
  implementation report on whether the codebase is reconciled with them.
